Rust gambling comes with its own vocabulary, and half of feeling confident is just knowing what the words mean. Here's a plain-English glossary of the terms you'll actually run into, grouped by what they relate to — with links to the full explainers where there's more to know.

## Money and value

- **Skins** — Rust cosmetic items with real market value, used as the currency on most sites. What drives their price is covered in [what makes a skin valuable](/what-makes-rust-skins-valuable).
- **Conversion rate / spread** — the rate a site credits your deposited skin at, usually **below** Steam market value. That gap is a hidden cost.
- **Rakeback** — a slice of the house edge you generate, returned to you over time (instant/daily/weekly/monthly), usually with no strings. The one "free" perk with real standalone value — see [rains, rakeback and VIP](/free-coins-rains-rakeback-vip-explained).
- **Rain** — a periodic chat giveaway that drops a small pool of coins to active participants. An engagement hook, not a reason to pick a site.
- **VIP / loyalty** — a level system where wagering earns XP toward claimable rewards. It rewards volume.
- **Promo / referral / affiliate code** — a code that gives you a sign-up bonus and credits whoever owns it. How that works: [promo and referral codes](/promo-referral-codes-how-they-work).
- **Wagering requirement** — how many times you must bet a bonus before you can withdraw it. The catch behind "free" [bonus coins](/rust-gambling-bonus-wagering-requirements).
- **KYC** — "Know Your Customer" identity verification, usually triggered on larger [withdrawals](/kyc-on-rust-gambling-sites-explained).
- **Trade URL / Steam Guard** — your trade link and the mobile authenticator that make [Steam trade deposits/withdrawals](/steam-trades-deposits-withdrawals-explained) work.

## Fairness and odds

- **House edge** — the built-in mathematical advantage the site holds on every game. Explained in full: [house edge](/house-edge-on-rust-gambling-explained).
- **RTP (Return to Player)** — the flip side of house edge; a 96% RTP means ~$96 returned per $100 wagered over the long run (a 4% edge).
- **Variance** — how wildly results swing. High-variance games (jackpot, big upgrades) have rarer, bigger wins; low-variance ones bleed slowly.
- **Provably fair** — a cryptographic system letting you verify a result wasn't altered. Spotting a fake one: [provably-fair red flags](/fake-provably-fair-red-flags).
- **Server seed / client seed / nonce** — the three inputs to a provably-fair result: the site's secret seed (hashed up front), your own seed, and a counter that increments each bet so every wager is unique.

## The games

- **Case opening** — pay to open a case for a random skin. It's gambling, not "buying a skin."
- **Case battle** — players open the same cases; highest total takes everything. See [case battles](/rust-case-battles-explained).
- **Crash** — a rising multiplier you cash out before it "crashes." See [crash](/rust-crash-game-explained).
- **Upgrader** — stake for a chance to "upgrade" to a higher-value item at a shown win chance. See [upgrader](/rust-upgrader-game-explained).
- **Jackpot / coinflip** — pot-based (your share = your odds) and 1v1 games; the [rake is the edge](/rust-jackpot-coinflip-explained).
- **Roulette** — bet a color for a multiplier; the [green slot hides the edge](/rust-roulette-game-explained).

## The one rule under all of it

Whatever the term, the constant is the **house edge**: every game favours the site over time. Knowing the vocabulary helps you play informed — it doesn't change the maths. New to all of this? Start with the [five beginner mistakes](/rust-gambling-beginner-mistakes).
