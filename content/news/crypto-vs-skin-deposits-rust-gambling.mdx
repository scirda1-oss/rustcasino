Most Rust gambling sites let you fund an account two ways: with **Rust skins** or with **crypto**. They feel interchangeable, but they aren't — each has a different cost, speed, and set of strings. If you're choosing how to get money in (and, more importantly, out), here's the honest comparison.

## Funding with skins

Skins are the native option, and for good reason: Rust skins have **no trade hold**, so a skin deposit or withdrawal settles almost instantly once approved. No waiting days for a trade to clear.

The catch is **conversion**. When you deposit a skin, the site credits you at *its* valuation, which is frequently **below** the skin's Steam market value. That gap is a real, if hidden, cost — you're effectively paying a spread to turn an item into site balance. On the way out, skin withdrawals also tend to carry the higher fee band on sites that publish fees, and some sites limit how often you can redeem skins.

**Skins suit you if:** you already hold Rust items you're happy to convert, you value instant settlement, and you accept the conversion spread.

## Funding with crypto

Crypto (BTC, ETH, LTC, USDT and similar, depending on the site) skips the skin-conversion spread entirely — you're moving money, not items. Crypto withdrawals also tend to sit in the **lower fee band** where sites publish fees, and they clear in minutes rather than waiting on a Steam trade.

But crypto comes with its own friction:

- **Level gates.** Several sites unlock crypto withdrawals only after your account reaches a certain rank or activity level — so a brand-new account may not be able to cash out in crypto yet.
- **KYC on larger amounts.** Bigger crypto withdrawals are the ones most likely to trigger [identity verification](/news/kyc-on-rust-gambling-sites-explained).
- **Not universal.** Some sites are skins-only and take no crypto at all.

**Crypto suits you if:** you'd rather avoid the skin-conversion spread, you're okay clearing a level gate, and you want the cleaner in-and-out of money over items.

## The part that actually matters

Whichever you pick, the deciding factor isn't the deposit — it's the **withdrawal**. Getting money *in* is always easy; sites make sure of that. How, how fast, and at what cost you get it *out* is where they differ, so read the withdrawal terms **before** you deposit, not after you win.

A quick way to think about it:

- **Want instant and already have skins?** Skins — just factor in the conversion spread.
- **Want to avoid the spread and don't mind a level gate?** Crypto.
- **Site is skins-only?** Then the choice is made for you — weigh the conversion rate carefully.
